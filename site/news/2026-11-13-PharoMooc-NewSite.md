The website of the Pharo MOOC "Live Object Programming in Pharo" has been redesigned: [mooc.pharo.org](https://mooc.pharo.org)

The site is now a permanent, self-paced entry point to the course instead of a page about a specific session.

What is new

- Enrollment on France Université Numérique is permanent: register and start at any time, at your own pace. Quizzes let you check your understanding, and you can obtain a certificate.
- Every lecture of the 7 weeks has direct links to its slides and to its videos in English and French. Exercises and challenges link to their PDFs.
- The content of the MOOC is still fully valid for Pharo 14. The videos show an older UI, so some menus and tools look slightly different.
- A modern, lighter page: no JavaScript, a single stylesheet, and https links everywhere.

The course is free, dubbed in English and French, with subtitles in English, French, Spanish and Japanese. Since 2016, thousands of learners from more than 100 countries have followed it.
