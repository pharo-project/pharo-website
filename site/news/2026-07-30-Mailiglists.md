We have moved the Pharo Mailing lists to a new setup.

The domain is the same and we migrated existing subscribers. You should have gotten a mail for each of the lists that you are subscribed to.


If you had problems subscribing or receiving mails in the past, please try to subscribe again! The new setup gives us more means to analyze email delivery problems.
