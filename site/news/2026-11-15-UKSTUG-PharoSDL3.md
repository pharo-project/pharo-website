The next UK Smalltalk User Group meeting will be an online talk by Martín Dias on **Wednesday 25 November 2026**:

**PharoSDL3: New FFI Bindings for SDL3 in Pharo**

[PharoSDL3](https://github.com/pharo-graphics/PharoSDL3) is a new set of Pharo FFI bindings for [SDL 3](https://libsdl.org/), the cross-platform C library used by many multimedia applications and games. The bindings give low-level access to windows, input devices, audio, cameras, and GPU-accelerated rendering and compute.

The talk presents the design of the bindings and how they integrate with Pharo, followed by demos of a range of SDL 3 features to give developers a starting point for their own experiments. It ends with an open, informal discussion and Q&A.

This matters for Pharo itself: SDL 2 is now deprecated, and Pharo 15 plans to ship SDL 3 on all platforms.

## About the speaker

Martín Dias is a software engineer for the Pharo Consortium, working on graphical libraries such as Bloc. He has about 20 years of experience as a programmer, researcher and teacher, more than 15 of them with Pharo, and is one of the creators of Fuel, the object serializer widely used in Pharo, Squeak and other Smalltalks.
